# Alternative US-based suppliers (instead of PCBWay)

Context: the alternator brackets and the alternator controller (Marine Board) were both made through PCBWay. These are US-based alternatives. Most likely this means two shops, one for the board and one for the brackets, because no single US company does both at PCBWay's price.

## Marine Board (bare PCB + assembly)

- [MacroFab](https://macrofab.com) (Houston, TX): the closest US match to PCBWay's upload-and-quote model, including parts sourcing and assembly.
- [Advanced Circuits](https://www.4pcb.com) (Aurora, CO): a long-standing US board maker that handles 4-layer boards and filled vias, and offers assembly too.
- [Sierra Circuits](https://www.protoexpress.com) (Sunnyvale, CA): strong on tricky stackups and via-in-pad. It costs more, but the quality is high.
- [Screaming Circuits](https://www.screamingcircuits.com) (Oregon): assembly only, so pair it with a board maker such as Advanced Circuits.

## Alternator brackets

- [SendCutSend](https://sendcutsend.com) (Reno, NV): laser-cut and bent plate in aluminum or 316 stainless, with quick turnaround and instant quotes.
- [OSH Cut](https://www.oshcut.com) (Utah): works much like SendCutSend, with good bending and tapping options.
- [Protolabs](https://www.protolabs.com) (Minnesota): suits CNC-machined brackets with thick or complex parts.

## Notes

- Expect noticeably higher prices than PCBWay, often a few times as much for assembled boards. In return: faster shipping, no customs, easier communication.
- The Marine Board BOM uses many LCSC part numbers, so a US assembler would probably want some of them swapped for Digi-Key or Mouser equivalents.
- Existing PCBWay orders (W831004AS1P18 for PCB manufacture, T-1P19W831004A for PCB assembly) would need to be cancelled with PCBWay (Taylor) if switching.
