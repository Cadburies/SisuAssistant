TerraMaster F8 on Ethernet: Docker host for Signal K, Grafana, InfluxDB, MQTT Explorer, Sisu Nav and its tile server.

- **Reach:** Browser on Sisu → the service ports on `192.168.0.21` · SSH `scripts/f8-ssh.sh` (port 9222, LAN only)
- **Action:** agents deploy with `scripts/f8-deploy.sh` (rsync from the Mac; the F8 has no git/apt).
- **Needs:** on the boat's Sisu Wi-Fi/LAN · SSH via `f8_ssh_*` secrets (not reachable from Sisu-IoT)
- **Expect:** Ports 3000 (Signal K), 3001 (Grafana), 4000 (MQTT Explorer), 8086 (InfluxDB), 8087 (tiles), 8088 (Sisu Nav) answer.
- **Source:** `NETWORK.md` §4.2 · `OPS.md` §7 · `homeassistant/docker-compose.yml`
