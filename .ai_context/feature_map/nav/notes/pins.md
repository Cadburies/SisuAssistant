Map pins with name/description/URL, stored as Signal K note resources (shared with other SK apps).

- **Reach:** Sisu Nav → right stack → **Notes** → **Add note**, then click the map
- **Action:** add, view/edit or delete notes.
- **Needs:** on the boat's Sisu Wi-Fi/LAN with the F8 running; Signal K login
- **Expect:** Pins appear on the map and in Freeboard's notes; nothing saves while logged out.
