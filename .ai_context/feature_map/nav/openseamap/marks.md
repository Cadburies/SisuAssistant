Live buoys, lights and day-marks from OpenSeaMap over any basemap — not a chart.

- **Reach:** Sisu Nav → **Layers** (top of map) → **OpenSeaMap marks**
- **Action:** toggle on/off.
- **Needs:** on the boat's Sisu Wi-Fi/LAN with the F8 running; internet for forecast/online layers
- **Expect:** Marks appear from about zoom 9.
